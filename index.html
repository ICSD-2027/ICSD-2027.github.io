Welcome

Welcome to the International Conference on Stochastic Dynamics, hosted in the existing conference materials by the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science (AMSS), Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Succeeding the 2015 conference on the same topic at AMSS, this meeting will bring together experts and early-career researchers working in different areas of stochastic dynamics to exchange ideas, discuss recent advances, and foster new collaborations. In collaboration with a group of international researchers in the field, the organizers hope to develop this conference into a biannual international series in the future.

The main conference is listed for 21–25 June 2027, preceded by a two-day tutorial on 19–20 June 2027. Students and early-career researchers are strongly encouraged to participate.

Dates, location and other details in the current materials should be confirmed before publication. Submission, registration, venue and contact information is still pending.
