Welcome to the International Conference on Stochastic Dynamics, hosted by the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science (AMSS), Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Succeeding the 2015 conference on the same topic at AMSS, this meeting will bring together experts and early-career researchers working in different areas of stochastic dynamics to exchange ideas, discuss recent advances, and foster new collaborations. In collaboration with a group of international researchers in the field, we hope to develop this conference into a biannual international series in the future.

The main conference will take place from June 21–25, 2027, preceded by a two-day tutorial from June 19–20, 2027. Students and early-career researchers are strongly encouraged to participate.
