ICSD 2027 · Beijing, China

International Conference on Stochastic Dynamics

A meeting for researchers exploring stochastic dynamics, its theory and its applications.

Welcome

Succeeding the 2015 conference on the same topic at AMSS, this meeting will bring together experts and early-career researchers working in different areas of stochastic dynamics to exchange ideas, discuss recent advances, and develop new collaborations.

In collaboration with international researchers in the field, the organizers hope to develop the conference into a biannual international series. Students and early-career researchers are strongly encouraged to participate.
